Emergency stop

Uncheck to halt all Scout trading immediately after save — no new entries, auto-enter, auto-close, or pending-order processing. You can still close or void open positions manually.

Zerodha execution

Saved in the database — survives redeploy. When live orders are off, Scout updates the DB only (safe for testing).

Example: ₹20,000 balance with 90% cap and ₹2,000 reserve → max ₹18,000 deployed. New entries block until a sell frees capital.
